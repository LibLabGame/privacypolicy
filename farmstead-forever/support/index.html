Farmstead Forever

How can we help?

Email septogeddon@gmail.com for gameplay, technical, or privacy support.

For a technical problem, include the game version, your device model, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Delete local game data

  1. Open your device's Android Settings.
  2. Choose Apps, then Farmstead Forever.
  3. Open Storage (sometimes called Storage & cache) and choose Clear storage or Clear data.
  4. Confirm only if you want to permanently remove progress from that installation.

Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling also removes the game's app data. Delete any separate copies you made separately.

Start a new farm

The in-game new-farm/reset action resets your progression and creates a fresh local save. To remove the game's stored data entirely, use the Android steps above.

Online accounts

The current offline version has no online player account or cloud save to delete. An account-deletion flow will be provided if a future version introduces accounts.

Delete support correspondence

Send a request from the email address you used to contact us, with the subject “Farmstead Forever privacy request.” Tell us which correspondence you want deleted. Do not send an identity document. We will respond and explain any information we must retain for legal reasons.
