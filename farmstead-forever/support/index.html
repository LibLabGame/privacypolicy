Support and data deletion

Email septogeddon@gmail.com for gameplay, technical, purchase, privacy, or account support.

For a technical problem, include the game version (shown in the game under Farmhouse, then Me, then About), device model, and what happened. For a purchase problem, include the Google Play order number if available. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or payment-card details.

Delete local game data

  1. Open your device's Android Settings.
  2. Choose Apps, then Farmstead Forever.
  3. Open Storage (sometimes called Storage & cache) and choose Clear storage or Clear data.
  4. Confirm only if you want to permanently remove progress from that installation.

Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling also removes the game's app data. Delete any separate copies you made separately.

Start a new farm

The in-game new-farm or reset action resets progression and creates a fresh local save. To remove all data stored by the game on that device, use the Android steps above.

Delete the farm copy kept in Google Play Games

  1. In Farmstead Forever, open the Farmhouse, choose Desk, and turn off the Account switch so no new copy is saved.
  2. Open the Google Play Games app and go to its settings.
  3. Delete the data Farmstead Forever keeps with your Play Games profile.

Menu names vary by app version. Deleting the Play Games copy does not delete the farm on your device; use the Android steps above for that.

Delete an online account and online records

Send an email from the address or sign-in identity associated with the account, with the subject “Farmstead Forever account deletion.” Include your in-game display name if you set one. Do not send a password or identity document.

Request account deletion

After verifying that the request concerns your account, we will delete or de-identify the account, profile, and eligible online gameplay records. We may retain limited transaction, security, fraud-prevention, or legal records where required. Deleting an online account does not automatically erase local data on your devices; use the Android steps above for each device.

Delete support correspondence

Send a request from the email address you used to contact us, with the subject “Farmstead Forever privacy request.” Tell us which correspondence you want deleted. Do not send an identity document. We will respond and explain any information we must retain for legal or security reasons.
