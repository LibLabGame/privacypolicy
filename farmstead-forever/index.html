Farmstead Forever

Privacy policy

Effective September 14, 2026 · Current offline version

Farmstead Forever saves your farm on your device. The current version has no player accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising SDKs, or in-app purchases. It does not send your gameplay data to LibLab Game.

This policy explains how LibLab Game handles information relating to Farmstead Forever (Android package com.farmsteadforever.game), this information website, and support requests.

Information stored by the game

The game stores your farm progress, progression profile, gameplay counters, settings included in the save, and the time of your last session on your device. It uses these records to restore your farm and calculate offline progress. The progression profile is a local game record, not an online account.

The current game does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier. It has no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Local game saves are not uploaded to our servers.

Messages you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer you, investigate the reported problem, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Screenshots and save files may contain information you did not intend to share; check them before sending.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Google processes those messages as our email provider. We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence.

Google Play and this website

Google Play processes information about downloads, device compatibility, and store activity under Google's own policies. Google may make aggregate statistics and Android crash or performance reports available to developers. These platform services are separate from data sent by the game itself.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or external fonts to these pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages sites.

These providers may process information on systems outside your country. Their linked policies explain their handling and safeguards.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. We do not share game data with advertisers. Information you send for support is handled by our email provider and may be disclosed when required by applicable law. Local saves are stored in the game's application data area. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You can play without creating an account. Use Android's app settings to clear Farmstead Forever's storage to delete its local data, or uninstall the game. Clearing storage permanently removes your progress from that installation. Any separate copies you made must be deleted separately.

There is currently no online game account to delete. For help, or to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to us, contact septogeddon@gmail.com. Privacy rights may vary by where you live. We may ask for enough information to verify that a request concerns your own correspondence.

Support and deletion instructions

Children's privacy

The current offline game does not ask children to provide personal information. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's support correspondence or a privacy concern. If we learn that a child has sent personal information that we should not retain, we will take steps to remove it.

Future versions and changes

Online accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, advertising, and purchases are not active in the version described here. If a future version adds data processing, we will update this policy before enabling it and provide any required notices or choices. The effective date above identifies the latest policy revision.
