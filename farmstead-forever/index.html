Farmstead Forever

Privacy policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Farmstead Forever stores your farm on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game can also keep a copy of your farm in your Google account. Purchases made through Google Play are checked with Google by our server before they are delivered. The game may show contextual ads through Google AdMob and downloads a small settings file from this website. This policy explains what those features process and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to Farmstead Forever (Android package com.farmsteadforever.game), its online services, this information website, and support requests operated by LibLab Game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores farm progress, your progression profile, gameplay counters including total time played, settings, ad-frequency counters, purchase entitlements, processed purchase tokens, the time of your last session, and the most recent ad-frequency settings downloaded from this website. It uses these records to restore your farm, calculate offline progress, prevent duplicate purchase grants, and respect ad limits. You can play as a guest and keep normal play and saves local.

Google Play Games: sign-in and cloud save

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name, and may sign you in automatically on later visits. If you turn on the cloud copy with the Account switch in the game, a compressed copy of your farm save is stored in your Google account through Play Games Saved Games, so the farm can be brought to another device. Badges you earn in the game may also be recorded as achievements on your Play Games profile. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy.

You can turn the cloud copy off in the game at any time. You can delete what the game keeps with your Play Games profile from the Google Play Games app's settings.

Other optional online features

Some optional online features, such as leaderboards, may use Supabase, and not all of them are available yet. If you choose to enable one, Supabase may process an account identifier and the information required by the sign-in method, a player profile or display name, leaderboard scores, challenge records, and service-security data such as IP address and request logs. We use this information to authenticate you, provide the feature, prevent abuse, and diagnose service problems.

Guests do not need an online account. Access controls are designed to limit private records to the authenticated owner. Public leaderboard entries use a display name and score rather than exposing an account ID.

Advertising

Farmstead Forever may use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded ads and limited interstitial ads. Google and participating ad providers may process device and app information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app identifiers where permitted, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising. The game does not access the Android advertising ID.

The game is intended for players aged 9 and older. Ad requests for children or users whose age is unknown are configured for child-directed treatment, contextual or non-personalized advertising, a maximum content rating of G, and Google Play Families self-certified ad sources. The No Ads purchase removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available when you choose to view one.

Settings downloaded by the game

The game downloads a small settings file from this website that adjusts how often full-screen ads may appear. The request contains no account, player, or advertising identifier. GitHub, which hosts this website, may log the IP address of the request as it does for any visit to the site.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and transaction under its own privacy policy. The game receives transaction information such as the product ID, purchase status, purchase token, and entitlement state so it can deliver and restore the item, prevent duplicate grants, and handle refunds. LibLab Game does not receive your full card or bank details.

Before an item is delivered, the game sends the purchase token and product ID to our purchase-checking service, hosted on Supabase, which asks Google whether the purchase is valid. That service keeps a one-way hash of the purchase token rather than the token itself, together with the product ID, the Google Play order number, the purchase status, and the purchase time, to prevent duplicate or fraudulent grants and to handle refunds.

Messages you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate the reported problem, and handle privacy or deletion requests. Please do not include passwords or payment details. Check screenshots and save files before sending them.

Support email is handled through Gmail. We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence.

Service providers

We use service providers only for the purposes described above. They may process information on systems outside your country and under their own policies.

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play, Google Play Games, AdMob, and Gmail)
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or external fonts to these pages. GitHub may log information such as IP addresses when serving the site.

Sharing, retention, and security

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers listed above, processed when you request an online or advertising feature, or disclosed when required by applicable law. Online records are retained while needed to provide the feature, protect the service, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. We use access controls and encrypted network connections, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You can play as a guest, decline optional online features, and skip rewarded ads. Use Android's app settings to clear Farmstead Forever's storage, or uninstall the game, to delete local data from that installation. Any separate copies must be deleted separately.

To delete the copy of your farm kept in Google Play Games, turn off the cloud copy in the game and delete the game's data from the Google Play Games app's settings. The support and deletion page gives the steps.

If you enabled another online feature, request deletion through the support and deletion page or email septogeddon@gmail.com. We will delete or de-identify the associated account, profile, and eligible online gameplay records unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, or applicable law. You may also request access or correction. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request concerns your account.

Children's privacy

Farmstead Forever does not ask children to publish personal contact information. Online features are optional. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional, and Google manages Play Games profiles for children, including parental approval through Family Link. Advertising for children or users whose age is unknown uses the safeguards described above. A parent or guardian may contact us to review or delete a child's online records or support correspondence. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child in a way we should not retain, we will take steps to remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the game or through another appropriate notice when required.
