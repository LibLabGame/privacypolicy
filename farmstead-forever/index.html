Farmstead Forever

Privacy policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Farmstead Forever stores your farm on your device. Optional online features may use Supabase for accounts, cloud saves, profiles, and leaderboards. The game may show contextual ads through Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing. This policy explains what those features process and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to Farmstead Forever (Android package com.farmsteadforever.game), its online services, this information website, and support requests operated by LibLab Game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores farm progress, your progression profile, gameplay counters, settings, ad-frequency counters, purchase entitlements, processed purchase tokens, and the time of your last session. It uses these records to restore your farm, calculate offline progress, prevent duplicate purchase grants, and respect ad limits. You can play as a guest and keep normal play and saves local.

Optional accounts and online features

If you choose to sign in or enable an online feature, Supabase may process an account identifier and the information required by the sign-in method, a player profile or display name, compressed cloud-save data, leaderboard scores, challenge records, and service-security data such as IP address and request logs. We use this information to authenticate you, synchronize an optional cloud save, provide cross-device identity, restore eligible entitlements, operate leaderboards, prevent abuse, and diagnose service problems.

Guests do not need an online account. Leaderboards, cloud saves, and other online features may remain unavailable until they are ready. Access controls are designed to limit private profile and cloud-save records to the authenticated owner. Public leaderboard entries use a display name and score rather than exposing an account ID.

Advertising

Farmstead Forever may use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded ads and limited interstitial ads. Google and participating ad providers may process device and app information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising or app identifiers where permitted, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising.

The game is intended for players aged 9 and older. Ad requests for children or users whose age is unknown are configured for child-directed treatment, contextual or non-personalized advertising, a maximum content rating of G, and Google Play Families self-certified ad sources. The No Ads purchase removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available when you choose to view one.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and transaction under its own privacy policy. The game receives transaction information such as the product ID, purchase status, purchase token, and entitlement state so it can deliver and restore the item, prevent duplicate grants, and handle refunds. LibLab Game does not receive your full card or bank details.

Messages you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate the reported problem, and handle privacy or deletion requests. Please do not include passwords or payment details. Check screenshots and save files before sending them.

Support email is handled through Gmail. We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence.

Service providers

We use service providers only for the purposes described above. They may process information on systems outside your country and under their own policies.

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play, AdMob, and Gmail)
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or external fonts to these pages. GitHub may log information such as IP addresses when serving the site.

Sharing, retention, and security

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers listed above, processed when you request an online or advertising feature, or disclosed when required by applicable law. Online records are retained while needed to provide the feature, protect the service, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. We use access controls and encrypted network connections, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You can play as a guest, decline optional online features, and skip rewarded ads. Use Android's app settings to clear Farmstead Forever's storage, or uninstall the game, to delete local data from that installation. Any separate copies must be deleted separately.

If you created an online account or enabled cloud features, request deletion through the support and deletion page or email septogeddon@gmail.com. We will delete or de-identify the associated account, profile, cloud saves, and eligible online gameplay records unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, or applicable law. You may also request access or correction. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request concerns your account.

Children's privacy

Farmstead Forever does not ask children to publish personal contact information. Online features are optional, and advertising for children or users whose age is unknown uses the safeguards described above. A parent or guardian may contact us to review or delete a child's account, online records, or support correspondence. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child in a way we should not retain, we will take steps to remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the game or through another appropriate notice when required.
